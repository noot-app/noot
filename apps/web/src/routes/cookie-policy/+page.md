## 4. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

### Types of Cookies We Use

#### Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function and cannot be disabled in our systems:

- **Authentication cookies**: Keep you logged in and maintain your session
- **Security cookies**: Prevent fraudulent activity and enhance security
- **Load balancing cookies**: Distribute traffic across our servers
- **Preference cookies**: Remember your settings and preferences (e.g., units of measurement, theme preferences)

#### Functional Cookies (Optional)

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:

- **User preferences**: Language settings, measurement units (metric/imperial), theme selection
- **Feature toggles**: Remember which features you've enabled or disabled
- **UI state**: Maintain application state across sessions (e.g., which dashboard view you prefer)

#### Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how users interact with our Service:

- **Usage analytics**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Performance monitoring**: Load times, errors, and application performance
- **Conversion tracking**: Understanding which features are most valuable to users

We use minimal analytics during beta to protect user privacy while improving the Service.

## 8. Your Cookie Choices

### Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings:

- **Block all cookies**: Prevent all cookies from being set (may break functionality)
- **Block third-party cookies**: Allow first-party cookies but block third-party ones
- **Delete cookies**: Remove existing cookies from your device
- **Receive notifications**: Get alerts when websites try to set cookies

**Important**: Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from using core features of the Service, including logging in and saving your data.

### Browser-Specific Instructions

- **Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
- **Edge**: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

### Mobile Devices

- **iOS (Safari)**: Settings > Safari > Block All Cookies
- **Android (Chrome)**: Settings > Site settings > Cookies

## 9. Do Not Track Signals
