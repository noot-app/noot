## What to Include

When contacting us, please include:

- **Your email address** (associated with your account, if applicable)
- **A clear subject line** describing your inquiry
- **Detailed description** of your question, issue, or feedback
- **Screenshots or logs** (if reporting a technical issue)
- **Device and browser information** (if relevant)

## Response Times

- **General Inquiries**: 1-2 business days
- **Technical Support**: 1-3 business days
- **Bug Reports**: We'll acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and provide updates as we investigate
- **Feature Requests**: We review all suggestions and will respond if we need clarification
