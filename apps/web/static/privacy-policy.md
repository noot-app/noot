## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Information You Provide

- Account & Profile: Email, (optional) name, handle, preferences, nutrition goals.  
- Audio Inputs: Voice recordings describing meals.  
- Text Inputs: Manually entered meal descriptions, notes, labels, event logs.  
- Biometrics (Optional): Height, weight, birth date, sex (for goal estimation).  
- Favorites, Labels, Events: User-created metadata.  
- Feedback & Support Messages.  

### 4.2 Automatically Collected  

- Usage Data: Pages visited, feature usage, timestamps.  
- Device & Technical: Browser/OS type, IP address, approximate locale, device identifiers (non-persistent).  
- Cookies / Local Storage: Session tokens, minimal preferences.  

### 4.3 AI & Derived Data  

- Transcriptions of audio (via third-party AI transcription providers).  
- Structured nutrition estimates (macros, micronutrients).  
- Aggregated daily/weekly summaries, percentage-of-goal calculations.  
- AI-generated analysis or qualitative suggestions (“insights”).

### 4.4 Sensitive or Health Information  

Do not submit protected health information (PHI). We are NOT a HIPAA covered entity or Business Associate. Nutrition data you enter is for self-tracking only and is not medical data processing under regulated frameworks. If you submit more sensitive health data than intended, you do so at your own risk and may request deletion.
