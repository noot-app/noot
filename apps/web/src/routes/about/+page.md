## Our Mission

Noot is an AI-powered nutrition tracking platform designed to make healthy eating effortless and accessible. Our mission is to simplify nutrition tracking through voice-first technology, making it easy for anyone to understand and improve their dietary habits.

## What We Do

We provide:

- **Voice-First Tracking**: Speak your meals naturally, and our AI understands what you ate
- **Intelligent Analysis**: Automatic nutritional breakdown with calories, macros, and micronutrients
- **Personalized Insights**: Daily, weekly, and monthly summaries tailored to your goals
- **Privacy-Focused**: Your data belongs to you, with transparency at every step

## Beta Status
